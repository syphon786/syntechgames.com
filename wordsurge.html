How to play WordSurge

Easy to learn. Hard to put down.

Steer the falling piece

Tap left or right of the piece, anywhere on that side, to move it a column. Swipe up to rotate. Drag down slowly to guide it, or flick down to drop it into place.

Trace words to clear them

Drag across 3 to 20 letters in a straight line, in any of eight directions. Letters above fall in the pieces they landed as.

Chain words to Surge

Find another word within 4 seconds to raise your multiplier to ×10. At the top you Surge: it holds for 10 seconds and every word scores ×1.5 more.

Land on bonus squares

Letter squares multiply that letter; word squares multiply the whole word.

FAQ

Good to know

Is WordSurge free?

Yes. WordSurge is free to download and play, and is supported by ads.

When do ads show?

Never while you play WordSurge. After every third WordSurge or Daily Surge run, a full-screen ad may show when you leave the results. Where the law requires it, Google asks before ads are personalised, and you can change that choice in Settings.

Which words count?

Over 180,000 English words from 3 to 20 letters, in British and American spelling, including modern words like ZEN, EMOJI and SELFIE. Names, abbreviations and offensive words don’t count.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play every mode as a guest. Sign in to Google Play Games, with the profile you already have, to appear on leaderboards and back up your progress.

Can I play offline?

Yes, every mode except Compete works offline. Leaderboard results wait on your phone and send when you reconnect.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play Games on both phones. Your progress is backed up to your profile, and the new phone offers to restore it.

Why don’t I appear on the leaderboard?

Google keeps private Play Games profiles out of public rankings. Make your profile visible in Settings → Google → All services → Play Games → Profile visibility. The game shows where you would rank and how to change it.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. WordSurge is on Android today, and an iPhone and iPad version with Game Center is planned.

How do I delete my data?

Reset progress in Settings removes data on your phone. Play Games data can be deleted in the Google Play Games app. See our Privacy Policy for details.
