Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

SynTech (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") operates the WordSurge mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

WordSurge has no accounts of its own and we do not run servers that store your personal data. Most of what the App uses stays on your device.

a) Gameplay Data on Your Device

  • Scores, stats, achievements, XP and level
  • Game progress (Adventure, Mystery, daily puzzles, streaks)
  • Settings and preferences

This data is stored only on your device unless you sign in to a game profile.

b) Google Play Games (Optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games (or Game Center on iOS), the App receives your game profile ID, display name and profile picture, and sends to that service:

  • Your scores, for the leaderboards
  • A backup of your game progress (saved game)

This data is stored by Google (or Apple) under their own privacy policies. Your profile visibility setting decides whether other players can see you on leaderboards.

c) Advertising Data

If ads are shown, our advertising partner may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (e.g., Android Advertising ID, IDFA where permitted)
  • Approximate location (from IP address)
  • Interaction with ads and basic device and app diagnostics

Ads are personalised only if you agree in the consent screen. Otherwise only non-personalised ads are shown.

d) What We Do Not Collect

  • No email address, password or account
  • No usage analytics or crash reports
  • No payment details (purchases are handled by the app store)

2. How We Use Information

We use information strictly to:

  • Run the game and save your progress
  • Show leaderboards and back up your progress (if you sign in)
  • Show ads that keep the App free, and prevent ad fraud
  • Send optional daily reminders, scheduled on your device

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Tracking & App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS we comply with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.

  • We will request permission before tracking your activity across apps and websites
  • If you deny permission, we will not access or use the IDFA
  • Ads may still be shown, but they will be non-personalised
  • You can change tracking permissions anytime in your device settings

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following services:

  • Google Play Games Services (profiles, leaderboards, saved games) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Game Center (iOS) — apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google AdMob (Advertising) — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play / Apple App Store (purchases) — their own privacy policies

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Data is only shared:

  • With the services listed above, to provide their features
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect rights, safety, and security

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Data on your device: kept until you use Reset progress in Settings or uninstall the App
  • Play Games scores and backups: kept by Google until you delete them
  • To delete your Play Games data: open Google Play Games, then Settings, then Delete Play Games account & data
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under its advertising policies

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Withdraw consent
  • Object to processing
  • Request data portability

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Data Security

We protect your information with:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS) for all online services
  • Sign-in handled by Google or Apple; we never see your password
  • Tamper checks on progress stored on your device

9. Data Breach Notification

If a data breach occurs that affects your personal data, we will:

  • Notify relevant authorities within 72 hours where legally required
  • Notify affected users without undue delay where risk is high

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If such data is identified, it will be deleted promptly.

11. International Data Transfers

Google and Apple may process data in countries outside your own, under their own safeguards.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance.
